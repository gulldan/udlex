# Transfer to documents outside the anchor treebank

Fixed before any transfer outcome was computed. The question is whether a source order learned on one treebank helps on independently collected documents of the same language while dictionaries, corpus tables and suffix rules stay frozen.

**Frozen systems.** For the 66 languages with an official pair, reuse the hash-verified train and development inputs of the UD 2.19 fit without change. Each refit must reproduce the released order, training score and development counts, or the run is rejected. For a language evaluated by two folds, fit once on its pooled, deduplicated anchor (both folds) without a development gate. Engine 1.0.0, source tables, thresholds and cascade follow `order-219-protocol.md`. Nothing is tuned on a transfer corpus.

**Candidate corpora.** Every other treebank in the official UD 2.19 archive whose file prefix is the language's UD code, plus lemma-annotated corpora outside UD converted to the same layout and listed with URL and SHA-256 in `transfer-sources.json`. All splits of a candidate are pooled into one test set, since none took part in a fit. Annotation status is the `Lemmas:` field of the treebank README. *Gold*: manual native, converted from manual, converted with corrections, automatic with corrections. *Silver*: automatic, reported separately and never pooled with gold. Corpora without lemmas or with masked forms are listed as unusable.

**Overlap screen.** The reference is every sentence of all anchor splits, scorable or not. Remove a candidate sentence if (a) its NFC, case-folded, whitespace-free word sequence equals a reference sentence, or (b) a sampled 32-character window of its NFKC, case-folded, alphanumeric-only text occurs in the reference stream of an anchor file. Windows are sampled one in four by their own BLAKE2b digest, identically on both sides, and reference windows cross sentence boundaries so that resegmented text is found. Remove a whole document when at least 10% of its sentences are flagged. Then remove sentences repeated within the language's accepted transfer material, taking gold corpora before silver and each group in name order. Keep syntactic words with an observable form and lemma; masked tokens keep their positions. The ledger publishes every count and file digest.

**Source provenance.** A lexical table derived from a test corpus would hand one arm the annotated answers. For every source and every evaluation corpus, including the anchor test split, measure coverage of scorable word types absent from the fit's training split, and separately of such types that contain a digit or symbol (hashtags, codes, addresses), which a general lexicon rarely lists. Flag a source for a corpus when it covers at least 98% of at least 200 unseen types, or at least 50% of at least 30 unseen symbol-bearing types. Report the flags and repeat the primary contrast without flagged language-corpus pairs.

**Estimands and reporting.** Primary: paired change of learned versus declared order, macro over languages with at least 1,000 scorable gold transfer tokens after screening, pooling a language's corpora, with a 2,000-replicate paired bootstrap over documents (sentences where no document ID exists) within each corpus, seed 20261118. Also report pooled tokens, corrections, regressions, changed predictions, tokens answered by external lookup, the development-confirmed policy with its original in-domain decision, the remaining arms, every language and corpus, the silver corpora, the result without the 1,000-token floor, both rule settings, and the in-domain effect on exactly the same languages. Differences in lemma conventions between treebanks enter both arms on the same words and are not corrected.
