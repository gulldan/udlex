# Paired order on the 1.0.0 engine
Fixed before observing paired test outcomes. Audit all 172 catalog codes against the registered research TSVs, including national dictionaries, UniMorph, Wiktionary and enabled imports. Honor the registry's physical language aliases, per-language base priorities and original exclusions. Exclude quarantined or transductive inputs. Ignore previously learned overrides when defining the baseline. Each arm uses identical lexical bytes. The packaged one-resource dictionaries are a separate deployment configuration.

Use official UD 2.18 to keep the experiment independent of the 2.19 dictionary update. Use the catalog's main treebank if it has annotated training and a test split. Otherwise choose the largest training file (bytes) with a test split in the same language, with pathname as the final tie-break. Prefer a treebank with usable training over a test-only treebank. When none has training, retain a test-only entry for the roster and mark the effect unestimable. Remove raw and normalized sentence duplicates between splits and repeated evaluation sentences. Exclude shared document and sentence identifiers between splits, keep syntactic words with observable form and lemma and the engine's source-specific training placeholder policy. Keep the test denominator common across arms.

The primary learned order is the exact training optimum over permutations of *all* available dictionaries, with a copy fallback in its training objective. Minimize inversions from the declared order at equal correctness and use declared-index lexicographic order for any remaining tie. Compare on identical test sentences with the 1.0.0 ordinary-token full cascade, training-only corpus tables, locale-aware lookup, a common suffix table and fixed confidence thresholds (0,0). No entity path, user layer, target-dependent adaptation or per-arm retuning. The research cascade intentionally enables lexical lookup for all languages. Fit on train only. Secondary arms are best single source, greedy ordered subset, exact ordered subset and the learned permutation subject to a full-cascade development check (two-sided exact McNemar p ≤ .05, net gain at least max(5, ceil(scorable dev / 10000)); default to declared without development evidence). Develop and test on disjoint sentences.

Primary population: ≥2 sources, scorable training and test. Include unchanged learned permutations. Report language-macro and pooled effects, wins/losses, number of changed predictions, per-language counts and denominator of candidate languages without train or test. Use 2,000 paired bootstrap replicates resampling document blocks or sentences where document IDs are absent, separately in each fixed language, seed 20261118. The full-order and subset arms answer different questions. Transfer to independently collected 2.19 documents remains a separate study.
